Frequently Asked Questions

What is Amoguis Tropical Twist?

Amoguis Tropical Twist is your go-to place for delicious and refreshing milk teas made from the finest ingredients. We offer a variety of milk tea options, including fruit-based blends and classic favorites, customized to your preferences.

What ingredients do you use?

We use only the highest quality tea leaves, fresh fruits, and premium milk. Our goal is to ensure every sip is a burst of flavor and freshness, so you can enjoy the best in every drink!

Can I customize my drink?

Yes! We offer a variety of customization options, including sweetness levels, toppings (like tapioca pearls or jelly), and more. Feel free to make your drink as unique as you are!

Do you offer vegan options?

Absolutely! We offer plant-based milk options, such as almond and oat milk, for those who prefer dairy-free beverages. Simply let us know your preferences, and we'll take care of the rest!

What are your operating hours?

We’re open every day from 8 AM to 10 PM. Stop by anytime for your favorite milk tea or to try something new!

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes, we do! Our gift cards make the perfect gift for any milk tea lover. They are available at our store and can be used for any purchase.
