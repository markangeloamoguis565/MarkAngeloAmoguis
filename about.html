About Us

Welcome to Amoguis Tropical Twistt, where we bring you the finest and most delicious milk tea beverages crafted with love and care. Our passion for creating exceptional drinks is reflected in every cup we serve. Join us in our journey to revolutionize the way you experience milk tea!

Company Background

Founded in 2021, Amoguis Tropical Twist is a premium milk tea shop located in the heart of Phase 1, Mabalacat City, Pampanga. We specialize in crafting high-quality, handcrafted milk tea and bubble tea drinks made from the finest tea leaves, fresh fruits, and premium milk. Our mission is to provide a delightful and refreshing tea experience for our customers while ensuring top-notch customer service and sustainability in every step of our business.

At Amoguis Tropical Twist, we offer a wide variety of flavors, from the classic milk tea to fruity, innovative blends that excite your taste buds. Our drinks are made fresh daily, and we are dedicated to serving you the best tea experience possible. We believe in creating a community where tea lovers can gather, connect, and enjoy their favorite drinks together.

Our History

The idea for Amoguis Tropical Twist came about from a group of friends who shared a deep love for milk tea and a vision of bringing this experience to the local community. Our founders, who had spent years exploring different tea cultures around the world, were inspired to create a space where people could enjoy high-quality milk tea drinks with a modern twist.

We opened our doors in May 1 2025 with a small, cozy shop and a simple mission: to offer customers a place to relax, unwind, and indulge in their favorite milk tea beverages. Our store quickly gained popularity due to our focus on quality ingredients and the dedication of our passionate team. Today, we're proud to serve hundreds of customers each day and are constantly expanding our menu with new and exciting flavors.

As we continue to grow, our commitment to sustainability remains at the core of everything we do. From eco-friendly packaging to supporting sustainable farming practices, Amoguis Tropical Twist strives to make a positive impact on both our customers and the environment.
